# Story — the show behind the wall

## Act I: private weather

A quiet boy rarely finds a clean way into a room. In his head, feelings have rented the place without asking: Joy sells paper tickets, Anger keeps missing the downbeat, Fear operates a tiny fog machine, and Sadness insists on the slow song. Their show is loud; the boy is not.

## Act II: borrowed colors, chosen noise

Someone older shows him that clothes can be play rather than camouflage. He starts carrying colors that do not match on purpose. A guitar turns the private show into vibrations he can leave in a room. The feelings do not line up neatly; one of them keeps changing the lights.

## Act III: PSYLO

He walks onto a small stage with a shaking hand. The show escapes through the amplifier: not as a confession with a lesson, but as a strange, bright interruption. The boy is still nervous. Fear gets a microphone; Anger misses a cue and lands on the best beat of the night.

## Ending

The audience leaves. A paper ticket sticks to the floor. Behind the curtain, Joy is already setting up a second stage, and Sadness is trying on a silver jacket. Fade before anyone can call the show finished.
