# Research notes — hide reference and original decisions

## Boundaries and evidence labels

**Documented project fact:** the 18 titles below were supplied as research targets for this project. The current workspace's external network policy only allows approved hosts and does not include music-streaming platforms or hide's official biography/archive domains. Therefore no claims here are presented as verified biographical facts or as fresh source-by-source listening observations. The track-by-track entries are **listening questions / hypotheses**, to be checked by a person using an authorized release before making arrangement decisions.

**Interpretation:** short motifs, contrast, unusual timbres, memorable vowel shapes, scene changes, and live/electronic interplay are the creative questions requested for this study. These are not attributed quotes or a claim that hide used one single formula.

**Original decisions:** PSYLO uses a fictional protagonist and new lyrics, note sequences, riff cells, and preview sounds. It does not quote recordings, vocal tone, lyrics, or signature riffs.

## Fact versus hypothesis log

- **Fact in this project:** title, artist name, song target list, and creative premise are user-provided. No biographical statement about hide has been used to justify the fiction.
- **Hypothesis to test by listening:** a chorus may become more memorable when its vowel contour is simple while the surrounding timbre or scene changes unexpectedly.
- **Another hypothesis:** abrupt contrast works best when the pulse continues underneath it.
- **Not claimed:** any of the 18 songs follows these exact principles, or that these general observations summarize hide's entire method.
