# Chorus lyric drafts

All lyrics here are original and provisional. Each chorus uses four two-bar lines to give the eight-bar sketch room to breathe.

## A — 疾走型 / LONG O

> ポケットの宇宙　ひっくり返して  
> まだ知らない色　靴音にまぜる  
> 胸の奥で　ショーが始まる  
> **PSY-LO——！　光れ、変なままで**

The hook rises through **サイ** and holds **ロー**. The last line should feel like one breath that gets larger, while the lyric stays playful rather than triumphant.

## B — ポップ型 / DOUBLE HOOK

> 鏡の中で　紙吹雪が踊る  
> 似合わない色　今日は似合う  
> **サイロ、サイロ！　口笛みたいに**  
> **PSYLO! PSYLO!　まだ帰らない**

The hook repeats with a short final vowel: **サイロ / サイロ**. Repetition should turn the unfamiliar word into something easy to say without explaining its meaning.

## C — 変異型 / SWITCH & SHOUT

> 感情たちが　勝手に点呼  
> 青いバグから　赤い弦へ  
> 不安も怒りも　音にして  
> **PSY——LO!　次のショーが始まる**

The held **PSY** breaks across the electronic-to-guitar entrance; **LO!** is a short, high release. The final lyric opens the door to another show rather than resolving the story.

## Word and mouthfeel notes

- Spoken reading: **サイロ** /sa.i.ro/. The opening is two vowels moving quickly; the final Japanese vowel is an open **o**.
- **PSY-LO!** is useful as a visual split, while **サイロ** is easier to sight-read in Japanese lyrics.
- Compare a long **ロー** against a clipped **ロ** before deciding the final chorus form. Do not let English spelling dictate unnatural Japanese stress.
