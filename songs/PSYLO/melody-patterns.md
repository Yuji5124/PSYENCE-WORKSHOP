# Melody patterns — three eight-bar chorus trials

The browser preview's triangular tone is a pitch guide, not a vocal performance. MIDI numbers below use scientific pitch notation with C4 = MIDI 60. The drafts stay around B minor / D major color, but the bass and guitar can alter that impression. They are sketches to sing against, not locked toplines.

| Pattern | Tempo | Phrase behavior | PSYLO pronunciation | First decision to test |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| A — LONG O · selected direction | 160 BPM (150–165) | Short lyric phrases build through bars 1–6; the hook crosses the bar line in bars 7–8 | **サ** on bar 7 beat 3; **イ** starts on beat 4 and holds for about 3 beats; highest note F#5 begins **ロー** on bar 8 beat 3 for about 2 beats | Compare guitar driving through **イ** with a gap from the start of **イ** until **ロー** |
| B — DOUBLE HOOK | 136 BPM (125–145) | Backbeat and offbeat hats make a gently bouncing repeated hook | Two short **サイロ** calls, then two **PSYLO!** calls | Does repeating the word make it instantly memorable? |
| C — SWITCH & SHOUT | 148 BPM (135–155) | Electronic guide leads the first bars; sampled guitar enters at bar 3 | **PSY** stretches, **LO!** snaps high and short | Does the texture change make the last shout feel earned? |

## A hook backing comparison

The app keeps tempo, notes, lyric timing, guitar preset, and levels fixed between these two A variants:

- **RUN:** syncopated guitar keeps pushing under the three-beat **イ** and the final **ロー**.
- **DROP:** guitar leaves space from the start of **イ** in bar 7 beat 4 through bar 8 beat 2, then returns with **ロー**.

Both versions use a deliberately uneven drum and guitar pattern rather than the earlier straight pulse. The guide remains a synthetic pitch cue. A singer or licensed singing synthesizer must still test whether the long **i-o** sequence is comfortable and memorable at this pitch.

## Six electronic sound patterns

The browser offers six deterministic sound colors from Bfxr2's GitHub library. They are generated locally with fixed seeds, then placed at different offbeat positions across the eight bars: **Alien Handshake** (Signlr), **Buffer Skip** (Glitchr), **Power Short** (Zappr), **Reality Glitch** (Riftr), **Robot Choir** (Choirr), and **Alien Purr** (Crittr). Choose one to hear it layered into the riff; no recorded effect samples are bundled.

These pitches are new working material. The references in `research-notes.md` are listening prompts for broad construction principles, not melody sources.
